## 🥚 THE CHALLENGE BEGINS

*Stay focused. Catch the falling eggs. Keep your score climbing.*

</div>

**Drop Egg Game** is a colorful, interactive browser game built around a simple challenge: catch the falling eggs and aim for your highest score.

Featuring a playful chicken-themed interface, score tracking, a lives system, and interactive controls, the game combines visual creativity with hands-on JavaScript development.

Every round is a new opportunity to test your reactions, improve your performance, and beat your personal best.
